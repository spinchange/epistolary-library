*** START OF THE PROJECT GUTENBERG EBOOK THE HOUSE AT DUSK ***

CHAPTER I
The Lantern at the Gate

By the time Mara reached the gate, the last color had drained from the western sky.

She lifted the lantern, found the latch, and listened for any sign that the old house had kept its promise to remain empty.

CHAPTER II
Footsteps in the Hall

Inside, the corridor answered with a whisper before it answered with an echo.

Mara counted three slow footsteps above her, though no lamp shone from the upper rooms and no window admitted the moon.

CHAPTER III
Morning Terms

At dawn the house looked ordinary enough to be sold, abandoned, or inherited.

Only the note on the breakfast table suggested a fourth possibility: that it had been waiting for her by name.
