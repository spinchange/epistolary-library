Letter CL VIII 

Zelis to Usbek, at Paris. 

You are a thousand leagues from me, and yet you con¬ 
demn me ! A thousand leagues from me, and yet you 
punish me! 

When a barbarous eunuch lays his vile hands upon 
me, it is by your order: it is the tyrant who outrages me, 
and not the instrument of his tyranny. 

You may, if you choose, redouble your cruel treat¬ 
ment. My heart is at peace, since it can no longer love 
you. Your soul is debased, and you have become cruel. 
Rest assured that you are not beloved. Farewell. 

The Seraglio at Ispahan, the 2nd of the 
moon of Maharram, 1720. 


1 The tenth of 1754.
