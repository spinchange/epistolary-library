Letter I. 

USBEK TO HIS FRIEND RUSTAN, AT ISPAHAN. 

We stayed only one day at Koum. After having said 
our prayers before the tomb of the virgin who brought 
forth twelve prophets, 1 we resumed our journey, and 
yesterday, the twenty-sixth day since our departure from 
Ispahan, we came to Tauris. 

Rica and myself are perhaps the first Persians who 
have left their native country urged by the thirst for 
knowledge j who have abandoned the amenities of a 
tranquil life for the laborious search after wisdom. 

Although born in a prosperous realm, we did not be¬ 
lieve that its boundaries should limit our knowledge, and 
that the lore of the East should alone enlighten us. 

Tell me, without flattery, what is said of our journey : 
I do not expect that it will be generally commended. 
Address your letter to Erzeroum, where I shall stay for 
some time. Farewell, my dear Rustan. Rest assured that
