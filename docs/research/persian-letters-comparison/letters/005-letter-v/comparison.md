## Fresh translation draft

### Heading
**Letter V**

**Rustan to Usbek, at Erzurum**

### Translation
You are the subject of every conversation in Isfahan; people speak of nothing but your departure. Some attribute it to frivolity, others to some hidden grief. Only your friends defend you, and they persuade no one. No one can understand how you could leave your wives, your relatives, your friends, and your country, to go into climates unknown to Persians. Rica's mother is inconsolable; she asks you for her son back, whom, she says, you have taken from her.

As for me, my dear Usbek, I am naturally inclined to approve whatever you do; but I cannot forgive your absence, and whatever reasons you may give me, my heart will never accept them. Farewell. Always love me.

*From Isfahan, the 28th day of the first moon of Rebiab, 1711.*

## Comparison against Davidson

### Davidson text summary
Davidson conveys the social rumor around Usbek's departure and Rustan's affectionate reproach with little distortion. The letter remains simple, personal, and direct in both versions.

### Main differences
- **Frivolity vs giddiness**
  - French: `légèreté d'esprit`
  - Fresh translation: `frivolity`
  - Davidson: `a giddy spirit`
  - Davidson makes the phrase more idiomatic, but the French is a little more judgmental and social.

- **Hidden grief**
  - French: `quelque chagrin`
  - Fresh translation: `some hidden grief`
  - Davidson: `some heavy affliction`
  - Davidson intensifies the sadness beyond what the French directly states.

- **Rica's removal**
  - French: `tu lui as ... enlevé`
  - Fresh translation: `you have taken from her`
  - Davidson: `you have decoyed away`
  - `Decoyed away` adds a sly, almost conspiratorial nuance not required by the French.

- **Heart's refusal**
  - French: `mon coeur ne les goûtera jamais`
  - Fresh translation: `my heart will never accept them`
  - Davidson: `my heart will never appreciate them`
  - `Goûter` here is closer to inward assent than to intellectual appreciation.

### Tone comparison
- **French original**: affectionate, socially observant, and mildly reproachful.
- **Fresh translation**: tries to keep the simplicity of a friend's candid letter.
- **Davidson**: very close overall, though a touch more polished in emotional phrasing.
