## Fresh translation draft

### Heading
**Letter XXV**

**Usbek to Ibben, at Smyrna**

### Translation
I have received a letter from your nephew Rhédi. He tells me he is leaving Smyrna with the intention of seeing Italy, and that the sole aim of his journey is to instruct himself and thereby make himself worthier of you. I congratulate you on having a nephew who will one day be the consolation of your old age.

Rica is writing you a long letter. He told me he speaks to you at length about this country. The liveliness of his mind allows him to grasp everything quickly; as for me, who think more slowly, I am not yet in a position to tell you anything.

You are the subject of our tenderest conversations. We can never say enough of the warm welcome you gave us at Smyrna and of the services your friendship renders us every day. May you, generous Ibben, find everywhere friends as grateful and faithful as we are.

May I see you again soon, and with you recover those happy days that pass so sweetly between two friends. Farewell.

*From Paris, the 4th day of the second moon of Rebiab, 1712.*

## Comparison against Davidson

### Davidson text summary
Davidson is close throughout this short and affectionate letter. He preserves the basic sequence and sentiment with little distortion. Most of the differences are tonal rather than substantive.

### Main differences
- **Self-instruction**
  - French: `s'instruire`
  - Fresh translation: `to instruct himself`
  - Davidson: `to improve himself`
  - Davidson is idiomatic, but the French more specifically emphasizes education and self-formation.

- **Rica's letter and Usbek's report of it**
  - French: `Rica t'écrit une longue lettre; il m'a dit qu'il te parloit beaucoup de ce pays-ci`
  - Fresh translation keeps the immediacy of Rica writing now and speaking at length about France.
  - Davidson turns this into `Rica tells me he wrote you a long letter full of details`, which is smooth but slightly more paraphrastic.

- **Thinking slowly**
  - French: `moi, qui pense plus lentement`
  - Fresh translation: `I, who think more slowly`
  - Davidson: `I, whose mind moves slowly`
  - Davidson is elegant but a little more stylized than the plain French wording.

- **Friendship's present services**
  - French: `des services que ton amitié nous rend tous les jours`
  - Fresh translation: `the services your friendship renders us every day`
  - Davidson: `the services your friendship rendered us daily`
  - Davidson shifts the line slightly toward the past, while the French keeps the benefit alive in the present.

- **Recovering happy days**
  - French: `retrouver avec toi ces jours heureux`
  - Fresh translation: `recover those happy days`
  - Davidson: `enjoy once more those happy days`
  - Davidson is graceful, but the French image is more concrete: it suggests finding those days again with Ibben.

### Tone comparison
- **French original**: warm, modest, and intimate.
- **Fresh translation**: keeps the personal note and the small contrast between Rica's quickness and Usbek's reflective slowness.
- **Davidson**: very faithful overall, with only a light Victorian lift in diction.
