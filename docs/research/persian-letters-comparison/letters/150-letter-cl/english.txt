Letter CL. 

USBEK TO NARSIT, AT THE SERAGLIO AT ISPAHAN. 

Miserable slave ! you have in your hands letters which 
contain orders for prompt and strong measures; the 
least delay may reduce me to despair; and yOu remain 
inactive under an empty pretext! 

Atrocious things are going on : perhaps the half of my 
slaves at this very moment merit death. With this I 
send you the letter which the chief eunuch wrote me 
before he died concerning these disorders. Had you 
opened the despatch addressed to him, you would have 
found bloody instructions. Read these instructions, and 
execute them, or you shall perish. 

* * *, the 25th of the moon of 
Chalval, 1718.
